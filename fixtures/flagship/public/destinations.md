# Where we fly (hand-authored twin)

This file is written and maintained by a human — it has no `generated-by` marker, so ax records it
as the markdown source for `/destinations` and never overwrites it. Because it is committed, the
prebuild `ax manifest` run lists it on the very first build, which is what lets the dual-fetch
dogfood prove the middleware rewrite without a second build.

Ora Air serves 12 airports across 8 countries. Every route is bookable one-way.

## United States

- JFK — New York, John F. Kennedy International
- LAX — Los Angeles, Los Angeles International
- SFO — San Francisco, San Francisco International
- MIA — Miami, Miami International
- ORD — Chicago, O'Hare International
- SEA — Seattle, Seattle-Tacoma International

## International

- LHR — London, Heathrow (United Kingdom)
- CDG — Paris, Charles de Gaulle (France)
- TLV — Tel Aviv, Ben Gurion (Israel)
- NRT — Tokyo, Narita International (Japan)
- SIN — Singapore, Changi (Singapore)
- DXB — Dubai, Dubai International (United Arab Emirates)
